The Nova Energy System

Bits. Shards. Cores.

Nova Bits, Nova Shards, and Nova Cores are the three forms of Nova Energy planned for the Epic Nova universe.

Bits and Shards are being designed for future activities, rewards, collectibles, customization, and progression. Nova Cores represent the rarest form of Nova Energy and currently serve as symbols of support for Epic Nova and Project Nova.

Project Nova is the larger creative and development initiative behind Epic Nova's future game, community, technology, world-building, and connected player experiences.
