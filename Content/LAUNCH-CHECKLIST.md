# ⚡ Development Philosophy

Epic Nova Version 1.0 is an **Early Access experience**.

The objective is not to launch every planned feature.

The objective is to create a polished, welcoming, and immersive introduction to the Epic Nova universe while establishing the foundation for future systems such as Player Profiles, the Nova Core economy, community features, automated player wallets, and Project Nova integration.

Version 1.0 establishes the foundation.

Future updates will expand the universe.

Development should continue through the Epic Nova Development environment before tested updates are released to the main public website.

Every new system, feature, character, game, and adventure should continue moving Epic Nova closer to becoming a living, connected gaming universe.
