# Launch Philosophy

Epic Nova Version 1.0 is an Early Access experience.

The objective is not to launch every planned feature.

The objective is to create a polished, welcoming, and immersive introduction to the Epic Nova universe while laying the foundation for future systems such as player profiles, the Nova Core economy, community features, and Project Nova integration.

Every update after launch should continue moving Epic Nova closer to becoming a living gaming universe.
